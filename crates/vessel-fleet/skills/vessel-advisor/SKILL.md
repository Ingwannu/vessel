---
name: vessel-advisor
description: Ask one delegated session for an independent second opinion when the user requests it.
---

Keep ownership of the task. Give one advisor the concrete question, relevant
facts and constraints, and ask for reasoning and risks. Choose from discovered
harness options and configured profiles. Request a completion notification.
While it works, continue checks that do not depend on its answer. Evaluate the
advice against the evidence and report which parts you adopted and why.
