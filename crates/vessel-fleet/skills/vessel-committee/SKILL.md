---
name: vessel-committee
description: Obtain two independent analyses when the user requests a committee or a difficult planning review.
---

Write down the problem, evidence, failed approaches and the decision needed.
Discover available providers and options. Delegate the same bounded question
to two sessions with completion notifications and independent instructions.
Do not ask either to implement changes. Compare their evidence, resolve
contradictions with direct checks, and produce one actionable plan. Keep all
user limits on data access, credentials, resource use and publication.
