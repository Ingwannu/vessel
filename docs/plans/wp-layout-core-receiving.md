## Decision Log

- Purpose: check the previously bounded concrete-owner, scoped-composer and pure
  stop/render-identity producers together before anyone promotes a product caller.
- Existing constraint: their standalone tests cover different boundaries. A
  source-equivalent cherry alone cannot prove the combined callback/field borrow.
- Alternatives: enabling globals beside a second store, table-only Ready, a fake
  guard or manual visible claim would bypass the owner and admission conditions.
- Choice: keep the same `SessionResidents` and its literal test gate. Compose
  only the selected W1/W4/W21 owner foundations, B2 constructor leaf, pure stop
  kernel and canonical full-token Display. No moving owner branch is imported.
- Why: actual fields, prepared form borrows, canonical ticket consumption and
  full retirement remain in their one existing owners; presentation formatting
  exposes no extra allocator or transport capability.
- Consequences: this source still owes receiving strict/runtime and source review.
  It creates no product resident, actor, raw follow or Window-close authority.
